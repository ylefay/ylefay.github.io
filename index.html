Yvann Le Fay

About

I am a second-year PhD student in Statistics at CREST (Institut Polytechnique de Paris), supervised by Nicolas Chopin.

My research focuses on computational statistics, with an emphasis on Sequential Monte Carlo methods, variational inference, and scalable algorithmic approaches to uncertainty quantification.
